## S1: normalization/log-sidepath ablation

- **Task:** existing full-pilot model; LocalResidual network family, same 11 feature channels / same initialized parameters, same training batches per seed. Compare six encodings: `raw` (rectangular IQ + magnitude), `log` (log amplitude + regularized phase), `dual_global` (frame-RMS normalized IQ + frame log RMS, existing P2-B definition), `rms_global_only` (same normalized IQ, third channel constant zero), `rms_local_only` (circular five-tone sliding RMS normalized IQ, third channel zero), `dual_local` (identical local normalization with log local RMS in third channel). Both pilot and payload IQ are transformed in each arm. The auxiliary product, noise-variance feature and residual scaling stay fixed. Missing scale channel is explicitly a potentially lossy representation; source information accessible through other auxiliary features is noted.
- **Frozen budgets:** three new training seeds **34001, 34002, 34003**; 400 AdamW steps, batch 64, learning rate .002, weight decay 1e-4, gradient clipping 1, SNR uniform 0–20 dB, **train3 only**. Identical initial network state and training frames across all six arms per seed. Fresh evaluation seeds from disjoint 140 million range. Evaluate `train3`, `equal4`, `long6`, SNR **0, 10, 20 dB**, 512 fresh frames/seed/scenario, same frames across arms.
- **Primary descriptive contrasts:** at 10 dB in equal4 and long6, `rms_global_only` vs `dual_global` isolates the log RMS side input; `rms_local_only` vs `dual_local` isolates local log RMS; `rms_global_only` vs `rms_local_only` compares scale neighborhoods. Report ALL seeds, pooled BER and paired per-seed deltas, including negative results. No significance-based choice, no post-test tuning.

## S2: sparse pilot density, matched observations

- **Task:** still 32 subcarriers and one known pilot OFDM symbol, but reveal **only 32, 16, 8 or 4** equally spaced pilot tones; all missing pilot observations are physically treated as unavailable/masked. Pilot tone energy and noise variance per observed tone remain fixed: reducing pilot count reduces total pilot energy and overhead; note this confound rather than claiming a normalized-energy comparison. The payload remains 32 QPSK tones. No receiver, neural or classical, receives hidden pilot samples or test H. Cyclic prefix and frame-static channel as in P1.
- **Classical baselines with identical observations:** (i) periodic linear interpolation of sparse complex LS pilot values, (ii) Tikhonov-regularized delay-domain eight-tap least squares (`DFT8-ridge`) using **only observed pilot tones** with fixed noise-scaled ridge coefficient, (iii) Gaussian LMMSE posterior from **train3** covariance known from training distribution, (iv) a correctly matched **test-profile** LMMSE labeled `privileged_reference` and excluded from equal-information claims. At four pilots, an arbitrary eight-tap FIR is underdetermined; ridge/priors are essential. Add true H only as an evaluation diagnostic, never as model input.
- **Neural control:** small 2,060-parameter `res_equiv_local` from a fixed training-profile LMMSE coarse estimate (computed from observed pilots only), paired with that same train-prior LMMSE reference; separate model trained at each density on train3 only. Its base estimate, payload, and noise variance are the only inputs. No P2-E weights reused or retrained. The correct-prior LMMSE is a privileged reference for OOD profiles.
- **Frozen budgets:** new seeds **35001, 35002, 35003**, 400 steps, batch64, SNR 0–20 dB; train3 only. Distinct independent held-out RNG family starting 150 million; test three profiles × **0,10,20 dB**, 512 frames/seed/scenario, matched across all baselines and densities. Primary descriptive result: BER vs pilot count for equal4/long6 at 10dB, plus train3; report all methods, no presumed winner.

## S3: independent mixed-profile training-information route

- **Training:** two populations, **train3-only** and **mixture**. Mixture samples 1/4 each from train3 `[.72,.20,.08]`, equal4 `[.25,.25,.25,.25]`, long6 `[.40,.22,.15,.10,.08,.05]`, and two_tap `[.7,.3]` independently per mini-batch frame. **No test-only profile** in the mixture. Training seeds **36001, 36002, 36003**; same 400 steps, batch64, AdamW .002, weight decay1e-4, SNR uniform 0–20; matched model init, seed and underlying stochastic draws per regime to the extent possible, differing only in channel-profile selection. Models: `res_equiv_local` (physics constrained) and `p1_cnn_log` (comparison). Full pilots for this independent route; no P2-E checkpoint reused.
- **Fair classical comparisons:** each training regime shares its declared population covariance with its neural models. Evaluate ordinary full-pilot LS, fixed DFT8, **population LMMSE from train3 covariance** or **population LMMSE from the mixture covariance** (analytic mixture of the exact profile generators; equivalent to unlimited samples from that same training distribution, not test-specific oracle), and separately labeled privileged matched-test covariance for context. The mixture is non-Gaussian, so mixture-covariance LMMSE is the **linear** MSE-optimal estimator for that covariance, NOT the full Bayes optimum. The neural models also train on the full simulated paired input/bit distribution, which can be more informative than covariance alone; claims of perfect parity are not made.
- **Held-out:** new independent eval RNG family starting 160 million; `train3`, `equal4`, `long6` (the latter two now INCLUDED in mixture training), and genuinely held-out `late3` `(0.1,0.2,0.7)` and `eight_tap` `(0.34,.20,.13,.10,.08,.06,.05,.04)`. Test 0,10,20 dB, 512 frames/seed/scenario. Primary descriptive differences: mixed minus train3 BER within each architecture on held-out `late3`/`eight_tap` @ 10 dB; mixed-covariance vs train3-covariance LMMSE on the same frames. Also disclose performance on trained mixture members.
