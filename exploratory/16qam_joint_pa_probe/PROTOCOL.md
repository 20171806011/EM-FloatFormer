# EM-FloatFormer 16-QAM pilot/data receiver — exploratory probe

2026-11-15, frozen *locally before training*. Synthetic, civilian, receive-only communication only. No intentional interference, active emissions, radar, military data, or transmitter control. Distinct from the unfinished full P3-D (Sionna) phase. Existing P1/P2/P3 records are unchanged.

Question: Does a small (<3,000 trainable parameters) data-aware convolutional decoder gain BER over (i) DFT-8 pilot-channel estimation + standard 16-QAM hard slicing and (ii) a **training-data-calibrated** 16-QAM threshold slicer, on fixed, known synthetic mild/strong memoryless PA magnitude-compression conditions, with matched test information? A positive answer would be limited to this toy generator; it is not evidence of a universally superior receiver.

Generator: 32 QAM data tones, all-known random QPSK pilot symbol, 16-sample CP, static 3/4/6-tap independent complex Gaussian FIR channel, IFFT/FFT with orthonormal normalization for data, Rapp p=2 (soft amplitude saturation), AWGN at 10 dB referenced to uncompressed average symbol energy. PA compression acts on time-domain OFDM samples. Train on train3 only. Check clean identity, channel convolution equivalence, noise and Gray bit map separately.

Fixed training: 3 seeds (59101,59102,59103), 300 AdamW steps, batch64, lr=0.002, weight_decay=1e-4, gradient_clip=1, BCEWithLogitsLoss. Two identically initialized neural receiver arms: training only clean; training mixture of clean/mild/strong (uniform), same seed/frames and architecture; single small frequency-local circular CNN with <=3000 parameters, learned bit logits atop ordinary pilot DFT-8 channel estimate. Classical single global threshold is fitted on a **separate clean/mild/strong training mixture** and used without knowing test condition; the uncalibrated threshold and DFT hard-threshold channel estimator are additional fixed controls. Do not use test labels/true channel in predictors.

Frozen held-out: Profiles train3, equal4, long6, SNR=10dB, conditions clean, mild, strong (9 cells). 768 frames per seed/cell with independent test RNG; same channel, bits and noise used across receiver methods in each cell. 32 data tones * 4 bits = 128 bits/frame; 294,912 scored bits per receiver/cell across 3 seeds. Report per-seed raw bit errors and symbol errors, as well as pooled BER/SER. Unit of replication is seed (n=3); no post-hoc p-value hunting, no dynamic hyperparameter changes. Train-only calibration is independent of held-out test.

Controls: DFT-8 + fixed 16-QAM decision threshold; hard-threshold DFT channel estimate + fixed slicer; DFT-8 + train-mixed calibrated amplitude threshold (a stronger *non-Oracle* control). Known test-profile channel LMMSE not an equal-information comparator. Calibrated scalar-threshold demapper is not a complete model-based PA inversion/iterative clipping repair; results cannot claim to beat all strong classical receivers. All paths are passive synthetic and emit no waveform.

Validation: test pilot normalization, memoryless compression monotonic magnitude, exact noiseless propagation, BER scorer correctness, checkpoint save/reload, shape/finiteness and parameter count, paired scenarios, SHA256 manifest. Preserve negative findings; stop after evaluation and report. Do not commence P3-D/3GPP or other phases in this probe.

**Pre-training smoke-test correction (before formal model fitting):** a one-step pipeline smoke test accidentally used the initially proposed evaluation seed formula. Its BER prints were used only to validate execution, not for selection or tuning. Formal evaluation therefore uses the distinct held-out RNG family `990000000 + 1000*seed + 100*profile_index + condition_index`, disjoint from the smoke family. No training settings, methods, channel profiles, model architecture or sample budgets changed. The smoke outputs are not included in the formal analysis. This updated local protocol is frozen before the 300-step runs.
