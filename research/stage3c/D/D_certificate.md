# A finite-sample top-1 certificate for the released C4 readout

The object under audit is the actual [Stage 3B s17 lookup](https://github.com/20171806011/EM-FloatFormer/blob/cbf3d2c03f773731fdf1b0d695a63ec57cb62ca0/research/stage3b_math/A/c4_s17_lookup.json). Its published probability table was computed with 60,000 independent integration windows, seed 41117 and Hermite order 20. We take its decisions as fixed before collecting each forward sample. The two tasks are Q1 (relative symbol) and pre-pilot Q3 (received sector for a repeat of the first symbol) in C4 with known rate +0.35 rad/step, phase diffusion 0.10 rad per square-root step, 2 dB, and two consecutive time gaps of 2. The information is the pair of frozen 4-bit window codes from the actual s17 weights, and the public condition. Bayes optimality throughout means population top-1 accuracy at this information and under this simulator.

## Direct i.i.d. experiment

Write U and V for the two noiseless symbol indices at the initial phase, each incorporating the common uniform four-phase rotation. U and V are mutually independent and uniform because the two source symbols are independent and uniform conditional on that rotation. For each mother draw U,V, two independent normal increments A,B with mean 0.70 and variance 0.02, and fresh circular Gaussian receiver noise in every sample and window. The first, second and repeated third window directions are respectively i^U, i^V exp(iA), and i^U exp(i(A+B)). Each direction is divided by sqrt(3) before adding noise to each of its three complex samples. The real and imaginary components of noise each have variance 1/(2 SNR). The generator records the quarter-turn sector of the sum of the three noisy third-window samples. It calls the published encoder on the first two full windows. It draws the latent variables independently for each mother, with replacement. This gives the single-mother marginal law of the original C4 generator without the dependence of its balanced blocks. No hash collisions, cached duplicates, or permutations are used to inflate the count. Pseudorandom reproducibility uses the specified NumPy PCG64 seeds and batch size.

## Why 16 rows cover 256 cache pairs

Write each cache index as z=4r+j, with sector r and within-sector index j. Independent quarter turns a,b on the first and second windows leave the joint law equivariant. The construction can rotate U by a and V by b and rotate the circular noises of windows one and three by a, and of window two by b. The diffusion variables remain unchanged. The encoder's construction increments r by the same quarter turn and preserves j except on null boundaries. Q1 increases by b−a, Q3 by a. Therefore the conditional law of relabelled Q1=(V−U)−(r2−r1) and relabelled Q3=sector3−r1 (both modulo four) depends only on row c=4j1+j2. Every positive-mass row has 16 equiprobable full cache pairs, so a Bayes top-1 rule for the relabelled task is also Bayes optimal among *all* rules on the original cache. Each mother contributes one count to each task. No rotated copies enter the confidence calculation.

## Simultaneous bounds from the first sample

Let j(c,k)=Pr(C=c,K=k), and let d(c) be the released decision. Its excess error is exactly the sum over rows of max over k of [j(c,k)−j(c,d(c))]. For each rival k≠d let m=j(c,k)+j(c,d), and p=j(c,k)/m whenever m>0. The positive gap is m max(0,2p−1). Among N independent mothers the number M in this row having either label is binomial(N,m). Conditional on M, the rival count is binomial(M,p). If m=0 the gap is zero. Exact one-sided Clopper–Pearson upper limits U_m and U_p give the bound U_m max(0,2U_p−1), taking 1 as the upper limit for an empty denominator. The maximum of the three bounds gives a row contribution and their sum bounds integrated regret.

There are 2 tasks × 16 rows × 3 rivals = 96 comparisons. We allocate 0.035/96 to each rival-share upper tail, 0.005/96 to each pair-mass upper tail, and 0.005 total across both tails of the two fixed-rule accuracy intervals. The fixed-rule count of correct predictions is binomial because each evaluation mother is independent of the released decision rule. Bonferroni gives simultaneous coverage of at least 95.5% for this first family, including inter-task dependence. Reserving 0.005 for the separate confirmation below gives at least 95% coverage for the complete set of claims. Conditional confidence statements hold on the model, encoder and selected rules, without assuming that numerical integration was exact.

On 4,194,304 fresh mothers (seed 967193), Q1's fixed accuracy was 60.62944%, with interval [60.55729%, 60.70156%]; its regret upper bound was 0.005409 **percentage points**. Fifteen of 16 Q1 rows certified every rival strictly below the lookup's choice. Row 5 contributed the entire remaining allowance; label 0 had 9,662 hits and rival 3 had 9,415. Q3 accuracy was 63.36694%, interval [63.29578%, 63.43805%], and all 16 rows individually certified zero regret.

## Independent global confirmation

The second experiment was chosen after examining the first sample, with a separate fixed seed (967229), 16,777,216 fresh mothers and significance level 0.005 fixed before generating its data. The global null is that at least one positive-mass row and rival, for either task, has j(c,k)≥j(c,d). For each comparison condition on M as above and use the exact one-sided binomial p-value Pr[Binom(M,1/2) ≥ n_d]. It is valid unconditionally and conditionally when the rival's joint mass is at least as high as the chosen label's. For empty M use p-value 1. This implementation tests all 96 comparisons including any unobserved row. Under the global null there is at least one true component null H. Rejecting only if **every** p-value is at most 0.005 implies rejection of H, an event with probability at most 0.005. Hence the global p-value is the maximum component p-value, with no multiplicity divisor for this global conjunction. Individual or selectively reported discoveries would require different error control. Distinct fixed draws protect the confirmation from adapting to the first audit. The first-stage bounds remain usable if the global test abstains.

The global p-value was **8.54297 × 10⁻¹¹**, below the fixed 0.005 level for the conjunction. The weakest comparison was Q1, row 5: the released label 0 had 38,694 hits and rival 3 had 36,937 among 75,631 relevant mothers. Every rival lost on this independent sample. This gives a frequentist decision certificate for **both released top-1 rules at every cache pair** under the specified law, while retaining joint coverage of at least 95% for the full set of bounds and the global declaration. All 96 component results are provided with integer counts and log10 p-values. For extremely small p-values whose probability underflows in double precision, the log10 column retains the tail magnitude. The signed comparison of class masses uses population decisions as the estimand; no pointwise claim about the accuracy of the exported four probabilities follows.

## Interpretation and reproduction

This method certifies decisions at one physical condition, before pilot reveal. A correct maximizer can still assign inaccurate posterior probabilities, incur excessive log loss, or produce an incorrect language answer. Physics parameters are known at test time. Empirical coverage pertains to the stated probability model and independent sampling. It gives no guarantee for a different signal law, drift or unknown frequency offset.

Run from the repository checkout with NumPy and SciPy:

```sh
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python path/to/D/check_d.py --repo . --out replay.json
```

If this package has another path in the checkout, substitute that path for the script. `results.json` records both families' integer sufficient counts, bounds and seeds. The script checks the unchanged published LUT (normalizing line endings) and frozen weights with SHA-256 before reading the decisions.
